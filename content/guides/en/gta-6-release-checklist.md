## 1. Check your preload
Digital preorders could preload from **November 10**. Open your console library and make sure the download shows 100%. Details in [GTA 6 file size and preload](gta-6-file-size-preload.html).

## 2. Free up storage
GTA 6 takes **up to ~212 GB** depending on platform. Leave another 30-50 GB for the day-one patch.

## 3. Know your unlock time
- **PS5:** midnight on November 19 in your PlayStation Store region (US store: 12:00 AM ET).
- **Xbox:** one global store, so setting your console to New Zealand time unlocks the game around **11:00 AM GMT on November 18**.

Full city table in [What time does GTA 6 unlock](gta-6-release-time.html).

## 4. Turn on auto-updates and rest mode
The day-one patch downloads by itself if your console sits in rest mode with internet access.

## 5. Claim preorder bonuses
Every purchase made before **November 20** gets the **Vintage Vice City Pack**. Digital preorders also include one month of **GTA+**.
