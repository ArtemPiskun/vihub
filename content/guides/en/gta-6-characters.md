## The leads
**Lucia Caminos** is the series' first female protagonist. Fresh out of prison, she wants a new life but slides back into crime.

**Jason Duval** is Lucia's partner. He grew up around grifters and works for small-time players in the Keys.

## Supporting cast
- **Cal Hampton:** Jason's friend, a paranoid conspiracy theorist.
- **Boobie Ike:** a Vice City legend who owns a music studio.
- **Dre'Quan Priest:** a music producer.
- **Raul Bautista:** a veteran bank robber.
- **Brian Heder:** an old-school smuggler.
