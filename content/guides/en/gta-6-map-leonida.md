GTA 6 takes place in **Leonida**, a fictional state based on Florida.

## Regions shown before launch
- **Vice City:** the main city, beaches, nightlife and money.
- **Leonida Keys:** southern islands, boats and small-time hustlers.
- **Grassrivers:** swamps, alligators and wilderness.
- **Port Gellhorn:** a port town.
- **Ambrosia:** an industrial area.
- **Mount Kalaga National Park:** forests and mountains in the north.

## What comes next
After launch this page gets an interactive map with points of interest, hideouts and collectibles.
