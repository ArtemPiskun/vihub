## Short answer
- **PS5:** November 19, 12:00 AM in your PlayStation Store region. US store: 12:00 AM ET, which is 9:00 PM PT on November 18.
- **Xbox Series X|S:** the store is global, so the game unlocks at midnight in your console's time zone. Set it to New Zealand and GTA 6 starts **November 18 around 11:00 AM GMT**.

## Xbox unlock times with the New Zealand trick
- **US West Coast:** Nov 18, ~3:00 AM PT
- **US East Coast:** Nov 18, ~6:00 AM ET
- **UK:** Nov 18, ~11:00 AM GMT
- **Central Europe:** Nov 18, ~12:00 PM CET
- **Eastern Australia:** Nov 18, ~10:00 PM AEDT

## How to change your Xbox time zone
1. Go to **Settings → System → Time**.
2. Turn off automatic time zone.
3. Pick **(UTC+13:00) Auckland, Wellington**.
4. Restart the console and launch the game from your library.

## FAQ
? Does the time zone trick work on PS5?
! No. On PS5 the unlock depends on your PlayStation Store account region, so changing the console clock does nothing.
? Do I need to be online at launch?
! Yes. The first launch checks your license and downloads the day-one patch.
? When is GTA 6 coming to PC?
