## File size
According to GamesRadar, GTA 6 needs **up to ~212 GB** depending on platform. Keep another 30-50 GB free for the day-one patch.

## When preload starts
- **PS5:** November 10 at midnight local time in each region.
- **Xbox Series X|S:** November 10 at 12:00 AM GMT worldwide.

## How to preload
1. Preorder digitally on the PlayStation Store or Microsoft Store.
2. PS5: **Game Library → Purchased → GTA VI → Download**.
3. Xbox: **My games & apps → Ready to install → GTA VI**.
4. Leave the console in rest mode with internet access so the patch installs itself.

## FAQ
? Will it fit on a base PS5?
! Yes, but after system files and other games most base consoles have around 600-650 GB free. Clear space early.
? Can I play from an external USB drive?
! No. Current-gen games run only from internal SSD or approved expansion storage. USB drives can only store them.
