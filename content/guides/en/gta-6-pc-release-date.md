## Short answer
There is no official PC date. GTA 6 launches on **PlayStation 5** and **Xbox Series X|S** only.

## Why PC comes later
Rockstar has historically shipped PC versions months or years after console launches, as it did with GTA V and Red Dead Redemption 2. Nothing official has been said about GTA 6.

## System requirements
There are no official PC requirements. Any "GTA 6 PC specs" tables online are guesses. We will update this page when Rockstar publishes them.
