## Prices (US)
- **Standard Edition:** $79.99
- **Ultimate Edition:** $99.99

Prices in other regions depend on the local PlayStation Store or Microsoft Store.

## Bonuses
Every purchase made before **November 20, 2026** includes the **Vintage Vice City Pack**. Digital preorders also get one month of **GTA+**.

## Digital or physical
Boxed copies are **code-in-box**: there is no disc inside. If you want to play the minute it unlocks, buy digital and preload.

## Our take
Not sure? Start with Standard. The $20 difference is easy to spend later, once it is clear whether the Ultimate extras matter to you.
